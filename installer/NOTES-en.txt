VisiLens - Information before installing

VisiLens is a free and open-source program distributed under the MIT License.

It searches folders you choose for visually similar images. Similarity is not
proof that files are interchangeable. Please examine every group and keep
backups of important pictures.

Auto-Select only marks suggested files. Files are sent to the Windows Recycle
Bin only after you explicitly confirm that action.

Image scanning and fingerprint caching operate locally on your computer.
The "Check for updates" button contacts GitHub only when you use it.
The "Support me" link opens Ko-fi in your browser.

The Windows installer removes application files when uninstalled. User
preferences and image-cache data may remain in your profile. They are not
automatically deleted so that reinstalling does not unexpectedly lose them.
